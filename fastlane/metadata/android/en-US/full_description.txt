Restoid is a modern, privacy-focused backup solution for Android powered by the robust, open-source restic engine. Keep your apps, data, photos, and documents safe with enterprise-grade encryption and lightning-fast, space-saving deduplication.

Whether you want to back up photos to a home NAS, store encrypted snapshots in the cloud, or preserve full app data on a rooted device, Restoid gives you complete control without trackers, ads, or proprietary lock-in.

Root access is completely optional: back up custom folders, photos, and documents without root; root access is only needed to back up private app data.

Key Features:

Blazing Fast & Deduplicated
• Content-Defined Deduplication: Only unique changed blocks are uploaded, making subsequent backups blazingly fast and drastically more efficient than traditional tar.gz archives.
• Space-Saving: Multiple snapshots share common data, keeping storage footprints minimal.

Zero-Knowledge Encryption
• Enterprise security: All snapshots are encrypted with restic using state-of-the-art cryptography.
• Your passwords and encryption keys remain entirely under your control.

Flexible Storage Backends
• Local Storage: Save to internal memory, SD card, or USB OTG drives.
• Network & Cloud: Native support for SFTP (SSH key & password), REST server, and Amazon S3-compatible object storage (Garage, MinIO, Wasabi, Cloudflare R2, Backblaze B2).

Full App & Data Backup (Root Required)
• Selectively back up installed user applications.
• Granular control: Choose whether to include APKs, app data, device-protected data, external files, or OBB caches.

Custom Files & Media
• Back up personal folders: DCIM, Documents, Downloads, or any directory on your device.

Automated Background Schedules
• Configure recurring schedules (daily, weekly, or custom intervals).
• Smart trigger constraints: Run only while charging, on unmetered Wi-Fi, or when battery conditions are met.

Surgical & Safe Restore
• Restore entire apps or specific parts (e.g. restore data without overwriting the installed APK).
• Downgrade protection prevents accidental restoration of older versions over newer ones.

Zero Vendor Lock-in
• Standard restic format: Repositories use the native, open-source restic format. You can always access, inspect, mount, or restore your data on any PC (Linux, macOS, Windows) using the official restic CLI—even without your phone.

Maintenance & Repository Health
• Keep your repository compact with automated snapshot retention policies (prune old backups by day, week, month).
• Run repository integrity checks and clear stale locks with one tap.

Modern Material 3 Expressive UI
• Built with modern Jetpack Compose and Material 3 Expressive design, featuring dynamic theming, smooth animations, and edge-to-edge layout.
• 100% Free and Open Source (GPLv3), with zero analytics, telemetry, or third-party tracking.
